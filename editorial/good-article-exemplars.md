## Common traits these articles should teach the agents
- They answer the question early.
- They give the reader a practical next move.
- They use examples, numbers, and scenarios.
- They reduce confusion instead of adding abstraction.
- They feel trustworthy and grounded.
- They are readable without sounding dumbed down.
- They include clear steps, not just explanations.
- They help a stressed reader take action quickly.
