# Bad Article Patterns

Avoid these failure modes.

## Meta-writing
- "This section should..."
- "The article should..."
- "This guide will..."
- "The next move is to define..."

## Abstract filler
- long paragraphs that explain process instead of helping the reader act
- generic encouragement with no next step
- repeating the same idea with different wording
- talking about "frameworks" without showing one

## Fake usefulness
- article looks structured but gives no concrete plan
- no realistic numbers
- no worked example
- no real scenario
- no quick win

## Low-trust content
- factual claims without a source
- references to authority links that never appear in the article
- vague claims like "experts say" with no attribution

## Rewrite goal
Whenever a draft shows these patterns, rewrite toward:
- simpler
- more concrete
- more useful
- more grounded
