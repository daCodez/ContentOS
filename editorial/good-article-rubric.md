# Good Article Rubric

Use this rubric for writing, QA, and rewrites.

## A good article must
- answer the main question fast
- include a practical quick start
- contain a real step-by-step plan
- include at least one worked example with numbers
- include concrete actions the reader can take today
- avoid abstract filler and meta-writing
- sound supportive, specific, and useful
- include trustworthy external references when factual claims are made

## Hard fail conditions
- no numbers
- no example
- no step-by-step actions
- meta-commentary like "this section should" or "the article should"
- generic advice without a concrete next action
- factual claims with no trustworthy supporting source

## Strong article signals
- quick answer in first 200 words
- one cheat sheet or quick start section
- one worked budget or savings example
- one weekly or staged plan
- scannable bullets and headings
- practical FAQ
- realistic CTA

## Section standard
Every major section should include at least one of:
- a number
- an action
- an example
- a decision rule

If a section does not contain at least one of those, it is probably too abstract.
